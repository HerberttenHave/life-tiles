Google requires 12 or more testers who keep the app installed for 14 days before Life Tiles can go public. Send this first to collect addresses, and the second once the closed test is live.

## 1. Ask (now)

**English**

> Hi [name],
>
> Remember the tablet app I made for my mother's 89th birthday? It is now called Life Tiles and I want to put it in the Google Play Store.
>
> Google first wants 12 people to test it for 14 days. Would you be one of them? I only need the email address you use for the Play Store (Android). It takes you two minutes: install, open it a few times, done. Any feedback is a bonus 😉
>
> Demo: https://lifetiles.web.app/?demo
>
> Thanks!
> Herbert
